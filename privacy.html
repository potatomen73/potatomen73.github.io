Hub Drive Backups: Privacy policy

Hub Drive Backups is used by one person, its owner, and no one else.

It asks Google for one permission, drive.file, so it can store and read back only the backup files it created in the owner's own Google Drive.

It does not collect, sell or share anyone's data, and it sends nothing to anyone but the owner's own Drive. Its sign-in credentials stay in the owner's own private secret store.
